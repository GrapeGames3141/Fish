Queued traces reject left-handed saved profiles, a left snap before cock, a rightward snap after cock, left/off-axis hooks, pitch/YZ-only easing, and rightward easing. The corrected headless tests do not establish physical comfort or real device axis feel; that remains pending an intentional on-device session.

V8 is installed and proved cast/hook/lower operation, but no terminal marker was captured; its physical timing, terminal behavior, and sustained-fight haptic claims remain open.
