# Architecture

`src/domain/fishing_session.gd` owns deterministic game rules, explicit state machine, location-selected fish, quality-to-distance mapping, generic catch length/reason, and the continuous pull/ease fight. `fish_definition.gd` owns weighted location tables and each fish’s size, bite, tension, pulse phrase, and cadence. `motion_service.gd`, `save_service.gd`, `haptic_service.gd`, and `admob_service.gd` are replaceable platform adapters. `MotionService` samples gravity, acceleration, and gyro once per update, derives linear acceleration, and supports mirrored handedness: default right is `+X` cock/pull/hook and `-X` snap/ease; left reverses the signed axes. The learned 3D profile axis remains the primary live match while modest polarity gates prevent inversion. Rest/settle measurements establish noise, two examples produce a robust lower gyro reference, and only compact derived diagnostics are retained. During REELING, signed pose travel maps through a deadband and 100ms response to continuous `fight_load`; guidance markers do not gate progress. `SaveService` v4 persists handedness and selected location and intentionally invalidates a profile when handedness changes. `src/ui/main.gd` composes passive Android instructions, settings-only diagnostics/location pages, sensor-only fight information, desktop-only keyboard simulation, haptic dispatch, and capture mode. `tests/test_runner.gd` calls domain/adapters headlessly.

`HapticService` is an injected-emitter scheduler rather than a timer-only side effect. `start_fight` reserves a short hook-confirmation delay, then `update_fight` can schedule at most one bounded phrase at a time, leaving the fish’s configured quiet gap. Normal species phrases yield to a universal 0.8-second high (65%) warning cadence and a faster universal 0.5-second urgent red (90%) cadence, then reset to normal when tension falls. Terminal cues stop the fight before emitting their distinct result. Turning haptics off clears pending work, so no vibration can leak after a setting change.

AdMob integration is intentionally isolated: the adapter’s native-bottom contract returns a zero Godot reserve, exposes measured native height only for diagnostics, and safely handles absence of the singleton in desktop/headless tests. After UMP consent, `AdMobService` enters `sdk_initializing`, passes an `OnInitializationCompleteListener` to Poing, and requests the banner only from the first current completion callback. Injected initialization and banner-request callables let headless tests prove that early, duplicate, and stale completion paths cannot produce duplicate native views.
