## Full description (4000 max — 2,323 used)

```
Hooked is a fishing game you play with your hands, not your thumbs.

Hold your phone flat on your palm. Cock it back and snap forward to cast. Wait
for the bite you feel through the phone, pull back to set the hook, then work
the fight by raising the rod to gain line and tilting forward to give it back.
There is no cast button, no reel button, no tap-to-win. The rod is your phone.

FEEL EVERY FIGHT
Haptics carry the whole fight so you can keep your eyes on the water. The bite
is a two-pulse knock. Every species has its own pulse rhythm as it runs. When
the line loads past 65% you feel a warning cadence; past 90% it turns urgent,
and holding it there snaps you off. Ease the rod forward and the fish's own
rhythm returns.

EIGHT WATERS, TWENTY-FOUR SPECIES
- Willow Pond, New York — Pumpkinseed, Black Crappie, Brown Bullhead
- Pine Lake, Vermont — Bluegill, Largemouth Bass, Channel Catfish
- Cedar River, Michigan — Rainbow Trout, Smallmouth Bass, Northern Pike
- Hatteras Inlet, North Carolina — Red Drum, Spotted Seatrout, Bluefish
- Mangrove Flats, Florida — Common Snook, Mangrove Snapper, Atlantic Tarpon
- Cypress Bayou, Louisiana — Bowfin, Longnose Gar, Flathead Catfish
- Moonlit Reservoir, Arkansas — Walleye, Striped Bass, Blue Catfish
- Bluewater Offshore, Florida Straits — Mahi-Mahi, Yellowfin Tuna, Sailfish

Each water has its own bite delay, fight strength, size range and casting
lanes. A tarpon on the flats does not fight like a bluegill in a pond, and you
will feel the difference before you see it.

KEEP THE RECORD
Every landed fish is measured and written into your journal. Beat your own best
length for a species and the card says so. Your records are yours — no account
required to play.

BUILT TO BE FAIR
- Two practice casts teach the game your motion, not the other way around
- Left-handed mode mirrors the full cast, hook and fight contract
- Relaxed, Standard and Expert fight difficulty
- Reduced Motion option for calmer water and lighter animation
- Adjustable motion sensitivity and a haptics off switch

WHAT YOU NEED
A phone with an accelerometer and gyroscope, and enough room to swing your arm.
Play standing or sitting, but give yourself space — this game moves.

Hooked is free and supported by a single banner ad. No forced video ads, no
energy timers, no paywalled fish.
```
