# Willow Pond water-motion preview v01

Preview-only exploration of restrained, photoreal pond motion. It uses the approved 720 × 1280 Willow Pond LINE OUT capture as the visual source; the header, sky, trees, shoreline, rod, line, bobber, hand and reel are protected from the water shader.

Open the [fullscreen viewer](gallery/view-water-motion-v01.html), or review the [GIF](gallery/willow-water-motion-v01.gif) directly. The [WebM companion](gallery/willow-water-motion-v01.webm) retains fuller colour; browser recording makes it approximately 7.3 seconds, while the GIF is the authoritative six-second loop. [Frame 0](gallery/willow-water-motion-v01-frame-000.png), [frame 40](gallery/willow-water-motion-v01-frame-040.png), [frame 80](gallery/willow-water-motion-v01-frame-080.png), and the [motion mask](gallery/willow-water-motion-v01-mask.png) are retained for inspection.

The GIF is 720 × 1280, 120 frames, 20 fps, and loops forever. A hand-authored shoreline plus exact rod-alpha/protected-object mask confines a 1–3 px multi-direction refraction and small specular lift to the water. The GIF is encoded with Sharp's animated GIF encoder. It is not connected to the game.
