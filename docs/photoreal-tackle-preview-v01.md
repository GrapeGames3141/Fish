## Known concept limitations

- This is a narrow-panel diptych, not two independently composed true-9:16
  runtime frames. Actual portrait layout, safe-area placement, and rod/line
  anchors require later runtime review.
- The FIGHT panel incorrectly leaves the float above the water surface. In the
  real fight state, the float must remain submerged.
- It is material/style direction only, not pixel-exact or production-ready art.
