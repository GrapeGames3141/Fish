# Visual Review — Gate 1 UI checkpoint

The original code-native greybox captures are superseded for visual direction by eight inspected original 720×1280 UI captures: `ui-v1-loading.png`, `ui-v1-ready.png`, `ui-v1-bite.png`, `ui-v1-reeling.png`, `ui-v1-caught.png`, `ui-v1-records.png`, `ui-v1-settings.png`, and `ui-v1-synthetic_reserve.png`.

The new composition keeps the Pine Lake art full-screen, preserves a contrasty top motion hint, and keeps the active fight meter low and restrained. The rod, line, bobber, bite submerge, water reaction atlas, catch Bluegill, and journal all read as one warm teal/gold family. The settings/journal paths are visually distinct from the passive gameplay surface; no cast, hook, or fight touch affordance is present. The journal holds all six planned fish in one readable screen. The 88px synthetic overlay is clearly labeled test-only and is not native AdMob geometry.

These renders verify desktop visual hierarchy and runtime asset composition only. The installed v9 activity launched without a fatal/script-error marker, but secure lock/NotificationShade blocked a device visual capture; these desktop renders therefore remain the visual evidence. They do not validate device sensor comfort, foreground native-banner geometry, UMP, or physical haptic feel. The first ImageGen pass is authorized for integration but awaits human aesthetic approval before broader art promotion or release.
